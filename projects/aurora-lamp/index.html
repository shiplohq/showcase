02Anatomy

Six parts.
Nothing hidden.

Aurora is assembled the honest way: one screw, one joint, one plug. Scroll and the lamp separates along its own axis — or take the controls and do it yourself.

    03Light

    Choose your hour.

    Three temperatures, one dial. The room below answers the radio buttons — amber for the evening, noon for the work, daylight for the detail.

    2700 K220 lm — Ember

    The mode also lives on the base: one tap cycles, hold drops to ember.

    04Materials

    Materials that age in place.

    Nothing coated to look like something else. Aluminium dulls honestly, glass stays glass, graphite takes the fingerprints of a decade. Hover, focus or press a swatch to find it on the lamp.

      05Dimensions

      Printed to the millimetre.

      Desk-sized on purpose: tall enough to clear a monitor, small enough to live on a shelf between books.
