## What you may do

- Use the software and documentation in this repository under the terms of

- Make truthful *nominative* references — you may describe a derivative as
  "based on", "derived from", or "inspired by" a Shiplo Showcase project,
  and link to the original, as long as the reference does not imply
  endorsement.

## What you may not do

- Imply that a derivative is an official Shiplo product, is endorsed by
  Shiplo, or is maintained by or affiliated with Shiplo HQ.
- Use Shiplo names, logos or brand assets as the branding of a derivative
  product, in a product name, logo, or domain name, or in marketing that
  presents a derivative as Shiplo's.
- Redistribute Shiplo brand assets (logos, cover art, branded illustration)
  as standalone brand kits or as part of a design-resource library,
  unless the project's own README states otherwise for that asset.
